The world we're building

Start with the resident, and work back

Everything in this paper serves one picture: a Peabody resident whose home is safe, whose repair is sorted quickly and properly, and who is treated with respect at every step. If we get that right, we will have built the strongest housing operation in the sector.

A day in 2030

How it feels from the resident's side

A resident reports a leak from their phone. It is diagnosed in minutes, an appointment is offered that suits them, and the right person — a Peabody operative or an accredited local contractor — arrives with the right parts and fixes it first time. The resident is kept informed throughout and asked, afterwards, whether it was put right. It was. No chasing. No repeat visit. No uncertainty about whether their home is safe.

A home that is always safe. A repair that is always right first time. A resident who never has to chase.

That experience is not a single app or a single team. It is the visible tip of an operation that has to run flawlessly behind it — and that operation is what Peabody and Plentific would build together over the next five years.

Maintain your assets

The other half of the remit: the asset, not just the repair

A perfect resident experience is only half the picture. The same platform that looks after the resident looks after the asset — turning every interaction with a home into better data about it, and that data into control, compliance and trust. This is the shift from responsive repairs to full maintenance and asset management, on one platform.

A living asset record

Your data keeps itself up to date. Every repair, compliance visit and sensor reading refreshes the record as a by-product of normal daily operations — so the asset stays current because the work keeps it current, not because someone ran a survey.

Full observability and control

One source of truth for every home: accurate, reliable, up-to-date data with full visibility of KPIs and outcomes. Evidence for the board and the regulator is a click, not a project — always reportable, on demand.

Safety & compliance, one spine

Fire, gas, electrical, damp and mould and Awaab's Law run on the same asset record as repairs and planned works — not bolt-ons. One platform, one compliance picture, nothing falling between systems.

Radical transparency to residents

Because the data is trustworthy, you can share it. Give residents real visibility of their own home — its safety status, what has been done and what is due. Transparency you can only offer when your data is good enough to stand behind.

Why it holds together

The asset keeps itself up to date, so the data is trustworthy; because the data is trustworthy, compliance is provable and outcomes are always reportable; and because you can stand behind the data, you can share it openly with residents. Most providers cannot offer that last step — because they do not have the first.

Why now

The moment demands a different operating model

Four forces are converging. Each one, on its own, stretches the traditional repairs model. Together, they make the case for change unavoidable — and the opportunity, for a provider willing to lead, historic.

Residents expect more

Satisfaction is measured, published and scrutinised. Residents rightly expect the speed, transparency and respect they get from every other service in their lives.

Regulation is tightening

Awaab's Law, Decent Homes and the consumer standards turn repairs and safety into legally-timed, evidence-heavy work that has to be done — and demonstrably so.

Costs are under pressure

Rising costs meet constrained income and a growing repairs and retrofit backlog. Every pound has to work harder, and value for money is a duty, not an aspiration.

The supply chain is fragile

A fragmented contractor market, capacity gaps and failure demand make delivery unpredictable — the single biggest source of poor resident experience.

The status quo can keep the lights on. It cannot deliver a perfect resident experience at scale. That takes a new operating model.

AI at the core

The engine that lifts satisfaction while lowering cost

AI is not a feature bolted onto the side — it runs through the whole operation. It is what makes a perfect experience affordable, by taking cost and friction out of every job while making the resident's experience better, not worse.

Triage & diagnosis

Every report is understood, categorised and diagnosed instantly, so the right resource is dispatched with the right parts — the foundation of first-time-fix.

Smart scheduling

Jobs are matched to the best-placed operative or contractor and sequenced to cut travel, waiting and missed appointments — capacity used to its full.

Predictive maintenance

Patterns in the data — and signals from connected assets — flag failures before they happen and shift work from reactive to planned, the single biggest lever on both cost and disruption.

Compliance, assured

Safety and statutory obligations are tracked and evidenced automatically. For damp and mould, the entire Awaab's Law process is managed end to end — the case created, the right surveyor and remediation provider connected, and every step held to the statutory timeline.

Proactive resident comms

Residents are kept informed automatically at every step, in their channel of choice — removing the chasing that drives most dissatisfaction and most calls.

A learning operation

Every job makes the next one smarter — pricing, diagnosis and planning all improve continuously from a single, growing pool of operational data.

The double win

Traditionally, better service costs more and lower cost means worse service. AI breaks that trade-off: the same intelligence that gets a resident a faster, first-time fix is what removes the repeat visits, the wasted travel and the failure demand that drive cost. Satisfaction up and cost down, from the same engine.

Connected homes, preventative by default

The same platform can bring the home's own data into the workflow. Sensors on boilers, damp and mould, leaks and safety devices watch condition continuously, and when a reading points to an emerging fault or a rising risk, a preventative job is raised automatically — into the same orchestration. Problems are solved before the resident notices, and hazards are caught at the earliest possible point.

Smart Repairs & Compliance

Getting access to a home is one of the hardest, most costly things in housing — so the platform makes every visit count. When a repair booking secures access, it checks that property's compliance schedule for the next three months, and if a check is due — a boiler safety check, say — it automatically messages the resident: the home is due a check, and we will use the existing repair appointment to sort it at the same time. One trip does the work of two — because repairs and compliance run on one platform.

A supply chain that never fails

Resilience by design, not by luck

The biggest cause of a broken resident experience is a broken supply chain — a contractor that cannot attend, a specialism that is not available, a peak in demand that overwhelms the team. Orchestration turns a fragile chain into a resilient one.

No single point of failure

Work is never dependent on one team or one contractor. If capacity is short in one place, the network flexes — the resident never sees the gap.

Capacity on demand

A deep, accredited network sits ready around the DLO, absorbing peaks, voids surges and specialist works without long procurement or scramble.

Quality held to one standard

Every party — in-house or external — works to the same standards, on the same platform, measured the same way. Consistency the resident can feel.

A well-funded network

Contractors are paid quickly and fairly, so the best of them want the work — turning payment from a source of friction into a reason to prioritise Peabody's residents.

Planned, not reactive

The more work that shifts to planned and preventative, the less the chain is ever under stress — resilience compounds over time.

Full visibility, end to end

Peabody sees the entire operation on one surface — every job, every party, every SLA — so issues are caught and resolved before residents feel them.

Removing the inspection bottleneck

Inspections and surveyor availability are one of the most common causes of delay — jobs sit waiting while a surveyor is found and scheduled. Making inspectors instantly bookable, from both the in-house team and the external network, resolves the majority of these bottlenecks. And every visit is made to count: missing property data is captured as the inspector works, and job sheets are filled automatically — so the next step is ready before they leave.

The Open Framework

Traditional procurement locks providers into rigid, multi-year frameworks with a handful of large contractors — slow to set up, costly to run and closed to smaller firms. The Open Framework gives Peabody a compliant, open route to market instead. New contractors onboard and are accredited automatically — one digital, self-serve route that checks insurance, accreditations and compliance up front — so the supply base grows continuously, without a fresh procurement round each time. And every firm, from a local SME to a national contractor, is then procured in exactly the same standardised way — by direct award or mini-competition, job by job. One front door for all supply: open to local SMEs, lower procurement cost and time, and full compliance kept throughout.

Flexible awards, multi-tier backups

Work can be awarded however suits Peabody — job by job, or as bundled packages. And behind every award sits a multi-tier chain of backup contractors: if the primary cannot deliver, the next tier steps in automatically. That deepens the effective supply base and guarantees cover — so capacity keeps growing and the chain never stalls.

There are two familiar routes, and each forces a compromise. Build it all in-house, and you carry the cost, the technology risk and the supply-chain fragility alone. Hand it all to a contractor, and you give up control of your residents and your standards. The joint model is deliberately neither.

Peabody keeps

Control, residents and its own team

The resident relationship, the brand, the standards, the accountability, a growing in-house DLO — and ownership of its own data and decisions. Nothing about this model makes Peabody dependent or locked in.

Plentific brings

The platform, the AI and the supply chain

The orchestration technology, the AI, the accredited contractor network and the financing that makes it flow — proven capability Peabody would otherwise spend years and significant capital building alone.

Aligned by design

The partnership works because both sides win from the same thing: a better resident experience delivered more efficiently. Shared goals, shared measures, and an open model where Peabody's data and residents remain Peabody's — a genuine joint venture in outcomes, not a supplier contract.
